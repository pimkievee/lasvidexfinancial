Retirement Planning and tax minimization

A retirement plan is more than just saving. It’s a process of structuring your RRSP, TFSA, FHSA, and pension income into coordinated withdrawals so you can achieve the financial independence you want in retirement while minimizing tax impact.

Lesvidex will help you decide on retained earnings, corporate-held investments, and how to manage the mix of salary and dividends, which directly affects personal and corporate tax liabilities.

Building your retirement strategy with Lasvidex Financial

A practical and professional financial plan always responds to these questions,

How much do you need?

How do you get there tax-efficiently? And

How will you protect and maintain the wealth?

We are here to determine your retirement income gap, and optimize contribution strategies(RRSP vs TFSA vs corporate investment). Lasvidex will structure your withdrawals in retirement to manimize tax drag and avoid OAS.

Retirement Planning for business owners
